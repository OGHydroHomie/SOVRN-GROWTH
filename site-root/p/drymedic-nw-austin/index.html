Remediation protocol

From the IEP, 2 pages
  1. Build containment around Bedroom 2, the closet, the hall and the hall bath. 6 mil poly, a zipper door at the hall and a decon chamber outside it.
  2. Run HEPA negative air for the whole job and keep the work area under negative pressure.
  3. Remove drywall and insulation 2 ft past visible growth. Bedroom 2 north and west walls to 4 ft, the closet back wall full height, and the bath wall behind the vanity to 4 ft.
  4. Remove baseboard in Bedroom 2 and the closet. Detach the vanity for access.
  5. HEPA vacuum and damp wipe every surface. Apply antimicrobial to exposed framing.
  6. Clearance testing by the IEP before any rebuild.
