Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

SOVRN Growth, a trade name of Lux Eterna LLC, runs advertising and new-patient follow-up for dental practices. This page covers what we collect when you contact us, or when you reach out to a dental practice we work for, and what we do with it. Short version: we use it to answer you and help you book. We don't sell it.

SMS and mobile consent

Mobile information and consent to receive SMS will not be sold, rented, or shared with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number and email address.
  • What you tell us in forms, calls and texts, such as the treatment you're asking about and the times that suit you.
  • Basic site usage information, like pages visited.

How we use it

  • To answer your request and help you book with the practice you contacted.
  • To send appointment confirmations and reminders.
  • To run and improve the service for that practice.

We share information only with the dental practice you contacted and with the vendors needed to deliver messages and scheduling. We never sell it.

Health information

When we handle patient information for a dental practice, we do so under a business associate agreement with that practice, as HIPAA requires.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to provide the service and meet legal, tax and record-keeping requirements. After that, it's deleted or anonymized.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving texts.
  • Reply HELP to any text for help.
  • Email us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.
