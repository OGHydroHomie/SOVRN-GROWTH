Questions

What you'll probably want to know

Does she replace our front desk?
No. She covers the calls your team can't get to: busy lines, lunch, after hours and weekends. Your team handles everything else.
Which number do patients call?
Your existing number. We set up forwarding so she picks up only when your team can't.
Is it HIPAA compliant?
Yes. We sign a Business Associate Agreement with you before she takes a patient call, and she runs on a platform that signs one with us.
Does she book into our practice software?
She books into a calendar your team can see. During setup we check your software and connect her directly if it supports it.
Will patients know they're talking to AI?
If they ask, she tells them. Most callers want an answer and a time, and she gives both in a couple of minutes.
Can we change what she says?
Anytime. Tell Elijah what to change, and it's updated the same day.
