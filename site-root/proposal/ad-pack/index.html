Questions

What you'll probably want to know

What do you need from us?
Your logo, brand colors, five to ten photos of your office and doctors, and your implant offer: consult details, financing, and anything you want said or avoided.
Can you use photos of our patients?
Only your own patients' photos, with each patient's written consent. Otherwise we use your office, your team and product imagery like the samples.
Are these made with AI?
Some motion and product shots are made with AI tools. A person designs and checks every ad, and we never show AI-made people as your patients.
Will this work with our agency?
Yes. We deliver finished files and copy, ready to upload, so it adds no work for them.
What if we don't like them?
Tell us what's off when you review, and we'll change it. One full round of changes is included.
Can we get more later?
Yes. Ask for another pack anytime.
