Don’t Get Dental Implants Without Knowing These 5 Things

Implants can last for many years with good care, so it pays to ask the right questions before you start. Here’s what to know.

  1. 1

    Not all implants are made the same.

    Implants differ in materials, brands and how they’re placed. Ask which system your dentist uses and why.

  2. 2

    Your plan should start with a 3D scan.

    A 3D scan shows your bone and the nerves around it, so placement can be planned precisely before treatment starts.

  3. 3

    Ask exactly what the price includes.

    Some quotes leave out the abutment, the crown, scans or follow-up visits. Ask for one number that covers the whole treatment.

  4. 4

    Ask who will place your implants.

    Ask about the dentist’s training and how many implants they’ve placed.

  5. 5

    Know your payment options up front.

    Many practices offer monthly payment plans. Ask before you start, so cost never surprises you.
