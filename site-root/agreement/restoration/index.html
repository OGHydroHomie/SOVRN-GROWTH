SOVRN Growth

Installation Agreement

Emergency Dispatch and Dispatch + Revenue Recovery. Version of November 18, 2026.

01Parties and start date

This agreement is between Lux Eterna LLC, doing business as SOVRN Growth ("SOVRN Growth"), and the restoration company named at checkout (the "Company"). It starts the day the Company pays.

02What SOVRN Growth installs

Emergency Dispatch $3,500

  • Answers after-hours and overflow calls in the Company's name.
  • Captures the caller, the address, the type of loss and the insurance on every call.
  • Checks the caller is inside the Company's service area.
  • Alerts the on-call tech by text and call with the job details.
  • Keeps a record of every call and sends a summary each morning at 7 a.m.
  • Installation, testing with the Company's team, and 30 days of support after go-live.

Dispatch + Revenue Recovery $6,000

  • Everything in Emergency Dispatch.
  • Each emergency must be accepted by a person. If no one accepts within 5 minutes, or the window the Company chooses, it goes to the Company's backup, then to the owner.
  • Follows up on missed calls and failed handoffs.
  • Captures website inquiries and routes them to the right person.
  • Tracks open estimates until each one has a decision.
  • Connects one CRM or job management system.

Extra locations $1,000 each

Each extra office or market gets its own service area and on-call routing, on either package.

The package and number of locations are the ones selected at checkout.

03What the Company does

  • Within 2 business days of paying, provides its on-call rotation, backup contacts, service area and the questions it wants asked.
  • Opens or gives access to the phone, voice and CRM accounts in its own name, or chooses hosting (section 05).
  • Sets up call forwarding on its business line.
  • Makes its on-call tech and backup available for one test session before go-live.
  • Keeps its rotation and contacts up to date after go-live.
  • Pays usage on its own phone, voice and CRM accounts, billed directly by those vendors. SOVRN Growth gives the estimated monthly cost in writing before installation starts.

04Fee and payment

The installation fee is one-time and paid in advance at checkout. There is no monthly fee to SOVRN Growth unless the Company chooses hosting.

05Own it or we host it

By default the system runs on the Company's own accounts and the Company owns it outright. If the Company prefers, SOVRN Growth hosts it in a private account for $199 a month. The Company can move it to its own accounts at any time, and can end hosting with 30 days' written notice.

06Timeline

SOVRN Growth holds a kickoff call within 1 business day of payment. The system goes live about 7 business days after the Company completes section 03, or about 10 business days for Dispatch + Revenue Recovery. Delays in access or information move the go-live date by the same amount.

07The guarantee

Every agreed function is tested on the Company's line, with its team, before the Company relies on it. If an agreed function fails and SOVRN Growth can't fix it within 10 business days of written notice, SOVRN Growth refunds the installation fee.

The guarantee covers what the system does. It doesn't promise jobs, revenue or arrival times. It is the Company's only remedy if a function fails.

08Safety and responsibility

  • The system is not an emergency service. Callers who say they are in danger are told to hang up and call 911.
  • It does not give safety or repair instructions. That stays with the Company's team.
  • The Company is responsible for how it responds to each call and for its own licenses and insurance.
  • The system relies on outside phone and voice providers. SOVRN Growth isn't responsible for their outages, but will help restore service.

09Ownership and confidentiality

The Company owns its phone number, accounts, call records and caller information. SOVRN Growth keeps its own templates, prompts and methods, and the Company may use them for as long as it runs the system. Each side keeps the other's private information confidential.

10Ending early

The Company can stop at any time. Once installation work has started, the fee isn't refundable except under the guarantee in section 07.

11Limits

SOVRN Growth's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees the Company paid. Neither side is liable for indirect damages or lost profits, including lost jobs.

12General

Texas law governs this agreement. This page is the full agreement between the parties, and any change must be in writing and agreed by both.

13How the Company accepts

The Company accepts this agreement by checking the agreement box on SOVRN Growth's checkout page and paying. Checkout records the name, email, company and the date and time of acceptance. No separate signature is needed.
