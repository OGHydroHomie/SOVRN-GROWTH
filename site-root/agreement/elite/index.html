The terms

Version of November 21, 2026. Fourteen short sections in plain English.

01Parties and start date

This agreement is between Lux Eterna LLC, doing business as SOVRN Growth ("SOVRN Growth"), and Restoration Solutions By Elite LLC (the "Company"). It starts the day the Company makes its first payment.

02What SOVRN Growth builds

Elite Operations: a private system for the Company's owner, office team and crew chiefs, on phones and computers, with day and night modes. The package is the one the Company selects at checkout.

Core $10,000

  • Jobs and claims. Every job from first call to final invoice, with its stage, checklist, carrier and adjuster, and who it's waiting on.
  • Money. What's owed, what's past due, and how long each carrier takes to pay. Connects one accounting system, such as QuickBooks Online, if the Company uses one that allows it.
  • Crews and on call. Who's on which job, who's on call tonight, and who's out on a storm deployment.
  • Morning brief. Each morning the owner sees the things that need him that day, on the home screen and by email, each with a button that does it.
  • HR desk. The hiring pipeline, a new-hire paperwork checklist, certification and license renewal dates, on-call load, overtime, and review dates.
  • Homeowner updates. A private page for each job that the team can text or email to the homeowner in one tap, showing progress, who's coming next and the claim's status.
  • Ask Elite. Questions in plain English, answered from the Company's own records, with links to the records behind each answer.
  • Move-in. SOVRN Growth brings over the Company's ClickUp lists and spreadsheets, merges duplicates and flags missing details before go-live.
  • Found first. A one-time check of how the Company shows up when homeowners ask AI assistants and search engines for water, mold and fire help in its service area, plus the fixes SOVRN Growth can make to its business profile, website details and click-to-call links. It doesn't promise rankings.
  • Training and support. A live training session for the office team and crew chiefs, a recorded walkthrough, and 30 days of support with free fixes after go-live.

Complete $15,000

Everything in Core, plus:

  • Partner portal. A private link for each plumber, property manager and agent to report a loss and follow its status, plus a partner list with jobs and work sent, and an alert when a partner goes quiet.
  • Rebuilds and estimates. A list of jobs where rebuild work hasn't been offered yet, with estimate drafts started from each job's records, and follow-up on unsigned out-of-pocket estimates that includes the Company's financing link.
  • Reviews and proof. One review request to each homeowner when a job closes, and the Company's live review count, response time and jobs completed, ready to show on its website.
  • 60 days of support. Support and free fixes run for 60 days after go-live, instead of 30.

03What the Company does

  • Within 3 business days of its first payment, provides exports of, or access to, its ClickUp workspace and the spreadsheets it uses today, plus its team list with roles, its carriers and adjusters, and its open jobs. For Complete, it also provides its partner list, its financing link and its Google review link.
  • Opens the accounts the system runs on in its own name, or chooses the care plan (section 05). SOVRN Growth gives the list of accounts and their expected monthly cost in writing before the build starts.
  • Makes one person in the office available for a 30-minute check-in each week of the build.
  • Reviews each part within 3 business days of SOVRN Growth showing it.
  • Pays usage on its own accounts, billed directly by those vendors.

04Fee and payment

Core is $10,000 and Complete is $15,000, one-time. The Company pays the whole fee at checkout, or pays half at checkout and the other half when the core system goes live on its data. Paying from a bank account is preferred; a card is also accepted. There is no monthly fee to SOVRN Growth unless the Company chooses the care plan.

05Own it, or the care plan

By default the system runs on the Company's own accounts and the Company owns it outright. Or SOVRN Growth runs it for $499 a month, which covers hosting, backups, AI usage, monitoring and up to 2 hours of changes a month. The Company can move to its own accounts at any time, and can end the care plan with 30 days' written notice.

06Timeline

  • SOVRN Growth holds a kickoff call within 1 business day of the first payment.
  • Jobs and claims, money, crews, Ask Elite and the move-in go live on the Company's own data within 30 days after the Company completes section 03.
  • The HR desk, morning brief, homeowner updates and Found first follow within 45 days.
  • For Complete, the partner portal, rebuilds and estimates, and reviews and proof follow within 60 days.
  • Delays in access, data or reviews move these dates by the same amount.

07The guarantee

Every agreed function is tested with the Company's team, on its own data, before the Company relies on it. If an agreed function fails and SOVRN Growth can't fix it within 10 business days of written notice, SOVRN Growth refunds the fees paid.

The guarantee covers what the system does. It doesn't promise jobs, revenue, referrals, reviews, payments from carriers or search rankings. It is the Company's only remedy if a function fails.

08Employee, homeowner and partner information

  • SOVRN Growth keeps the Company's records confidential, uses them only to build and support the system, and deletes its own copies when the Company asks after handover.
  • The HR desk tracks the status of paperwork, licenses and certifications. The Company stays responsible for its employment-law obligations and decides which documents it stores in the system.
  • Homeowner pages show only what the Company chooses to share. Partners see only the jobs they sent.

09Ask Elite and automation

  • Ask Elite answers from the Company's records. Answers can be wrong or incomplete, so the Company checks them before relying on them for payroll, legal, insurance or safety decisions.
  • Nothing goes to homeowners, partners, carriers or staff unless someone on the Company's team chooses to send it, or the Company turns on automatic sending for that message, such as review requests or estimate follow-ups.

10Ownership and confidentiality

The Company owns the finished system, its accounts and all of its records. SOVRN Growth may reuse its general templates, components and know-how in other work, never with the Company's records or anything that identifies the Company. Each side keeps the other's private information confidential.

11Ending early

The Company can stop at any time. Once build work has started, fees paid aren't refundable except under the guarantee in section 07. If the Company paid half and stops before the core system goes live, the second half isn't owed.

12Limits

SOVRN Growth's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees the Company paid. Neither side is liable for indirect damages or lost profits, including lost jobs.

13General

Texas law governs this agreement. This page is the full agreement between the parties, and any change must be in writing and agreed by both.

14How the Company accepts

The Company accepts this agreement by choosing a package on this page, checking the agreement box at checkout and paying. Checkout records the package, the name, email, company and the date and time of acceptance. No separate signature is needed.
