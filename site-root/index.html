How much does a build cost?

It's a one-time fee, quoted on a 15-minute call once we see how your shop works. You see the full number, and any monthly running cost, in writing before you pay.

Do you replace Xactimate?

No. Most carriers expect estimates in Xactimate, so we build around it. Our tools draft and organize the work, and your team still sends the estimate from Xactimate.

What software can you replace?

Project boards like ClickUp, the spreadsheets that hold your jobs together, and add-on apps that don't talk to each other. On the call we'll tell you what's worth replacing and what isn't.

Who owns it?

You do. It runs on your accounts and your data stays yours. If you'd rather we host it, we can, and you can move it to your own accounts any time.

How long does a build take?

After-hours dispatch goes live in about 7 business days. Custom builds are scoped on the call, and you get the timeline in writing before work starts.

Is the after-hours line a person?

No. It's a voice agent set up with your company's name, service area and questions. If a caller wants a person, it takes their number and alerts your on-call tech. If someone is in danger, it tells them to hang up and call 911.

In short: SOVRN Growth builds custom software for water, fire and mold restoration companies, including operations dashboards, estimate drafts and after-hours dispatch. Each build is a one-time fee, quoted on a 15-minute call, and the company owns what we build. Based in the Austin area.
