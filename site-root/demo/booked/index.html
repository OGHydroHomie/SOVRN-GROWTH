Before your visit

The questions most people ask first.

What will it cost, and can I spread it out?

Your doctor will give you an exact price at your visit, after looking at your scans. Many people spread the cost with monthly payments, and you can see your options before you come in.

What happens at the consult?

You'll meet the doctor, get any scans you need, talk through your options and leave with a written plan and price. Plan on about 45 minutes.

How long does treatment take?

It depends on your plan. Your doctor will walk you through each step and the timeline at your visit.

What should I bring?

Your ID, your insurance card if you have one, a list of your medications, and anyone who helps you make decisions.

Bringing someone?

Many people bring a spouse or family member. Add their number and we'll text them the time and address too.

Running late or need to change something? Reply to our text and someone from the office will answer.
