The terms

Version of November 24, 2026. Fourteen short sections in plain English.

01Parties and start date

This agreement is between Lux Eterna LLC, doing business as SOVRN Growth ("SOVRN Growth"), and the business that operates DRYmedic Restoration of NW Austin, as named at checkout (the "Company"). It starts the day the Company makes its first payment.

02What SOVRN Growth builds

The Mold Estimate Desk: a private web app for the Company's owner and team, on phones and computers, that turns a mold job's files into a draft estimate the Company reviews and enters into Xactimate.

  • Job intake. The Company drops in a job's site and moisture photos and its assessment report and protocol, downloaded from DASH, the sketch, exported from Xactimate as an ESX file or PDF, and any video from a phone.
  • Photos sorted by room. Each photo is matched to a room on the sketch, moisture photos are grouped with their room and their readings are logged, and each room's photos are ready to use as a set, such as for a TPA file.
  • Draft estimate. Room sizes are read from the sketch and matched to the scope in the protocol. Each line item, its quantity and the reason for it are drafted in the Company's own line items, wording and order, covering containment, removal, HEPA, antimicrobial, clearance and the other lines the protocol calls for.
  • Flags. Anything the desk can't confirm, such as a room with no photos, a reading it can't read or a line the protocol doesn't support, is flagged on the draft.
  • The Company's templates. SOVRN Growth sets up the Company's common line items, wording and order from the finished estimates the Company shares.
  • Changes from the walkthrough. The changes the Company asks for on the walkthrough call, within the scope of this section.
  • Training and support. A walkthrough for the Company's team, and 30 days of support with free fixes after the first payment.

If Cotality, which makes DASH, approves SOVRN Growth to connect to the Company's DASH account, SOVRN Growth connects it so new jobs come over on their own. That approval isn't in SOVRN Growth's control. Until then, jobs come in by download, which takes about a minute per job.

Anything beyond this section is quoted in writing before any work on it starts.

03What the Company does

  • Within 3 business days of its first payment, shares 3 to 5 finished mold jobs, each with its photos, assessment report and protocol, sketch, and the estimate it sent. Homeowner names and addresses can be blacked out.
  • Lists the changes it wants on the walkthrough call, or in writing within 1 business day after it.
  • Makes one person available for the follow-up call.
  • Reviews every draft before it goes into Xactimate or to anyone else.
  • If it moves the desk to its own accounts after day 30, opens them in its own name. SOVRN Growth gives the list of accounts and their expected monthly cost in writing first.

04Fee and payment

The fee is $5,000, one-time. The Company pays it in one payment at checkout, or pays $2,500 at checkout and $2,500 thirty days later. SOVRN Growth sends the second payment as an invoice on day 30, due on receipt. If it's more than 10 days late, SOVRN Growth may pause the desk until it's paid. Paying from a bank account is preferred; a card is also accepted. There is no monthly fee to SOVRN Growth unless the Company chooses the care plan in section 05.

05Hosting, or the care plan

SOVRN Growth hosts the desk at no charge for the first 30 days after the first payment. After that, the Company either moves it to its own accounts and pays their usage directly, billed by those vendors, or SOVRN Growth keeps running it for $199 a month, which covers hosting, backups, AI usage, monitoring and up to 1 hour of changes a month. The Company can move to its own accounts at any time, and can end the care plan with 30 days' written notice.

06Timeline

  • The desk is shown working on a mold job on the walkthrough call, before the Company pays anything.
  • SOVRN Growth delivers the changes from the walkthrough within 72 hours of the first payment, and reviews them with the Company on a follow-up call.
  • The Company's templates are set up within 3 business days of receiving its finished jobs.
  • Delays in files or feedback move these dates by the same amount.

07The guarantees

  • It works. Every function in section 02 is tested on the Company's own jobs before the Company relies on it. If one fails and SOVRN Growth can't fix it within 10 business days of written notice, SOVRN Growth refunds the fees paid.
  • At least 5x. If the desk hasn't returned at least 5x the fee within 12 months of the first payment, SOVRN Growth keeps building improvements to it at no charge until it does. The return counts the estimating time it saves and the mold jobs it helps win, using numbers the Company and SOVRN Growth agree in writing on the follow-up call.

The guarantees cover what the desk does. They don't promise jobs, revenue, or acceptance by any carrier, TPA or homeowner. The refund above is the Company's only remedy if a function fails.

08Job files and homeowner information

SOVRN Growth keeps the Company's files confidential, uses them only to build, run and support the Company's desk, never sells or shares them, and deletes its own copies when the Company asks after handover. The desk only reads what the Company drops in or connects. It never writes to DASH or Xactimate.

09Drafts and accuracy

The desk drafts and the Company decides. Drafts can be wrong or incomplete, so the Company checks each one before it goes into Xactimate or to a homeowner, carrier or TPA. Nothing goes to anyone unless someone on the Company's team sends it.

10Ownership and confidentiality

Once the fee is paid in full, the Company owns the desk, its accounts and all of its records. Until then, the Company has full use of it. SOVRN Growth may reuse its general templates, components and know-how in other work, never with the Company's files or anything that identifies the Company. Each side keeps the other's private information confidential.

11Ending early

The Company can stop using the desk at any time. Fees paid aren't refundable except under the guarantee in section 07. On the half-now option, the second $2,500 is still due on day 30.

12Limits

SOVRN Growth's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees the Company paid. Neither side is liable for indirect damages or lost profits, including lost jobs.

13General

Texas law governs this agreement. This page is the full agreement between the parties, and any change must be in writing and agreed by both.

14How the Company accepts

The Company accepts this agreement by choosing how to pay on this page, checking the agreement box at checkout and paying. Checkout records the payment option, the name, email, company and the date and time of acceptance. No separate signature is needed.
