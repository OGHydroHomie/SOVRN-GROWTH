Questions worth answering before you buy

Does this replace our number or our staff?

No automatic replacement is assumed. We design around your approved routing and existing team, and verify phone-number compatibility before activation.

Can I see exactly what callers said?

Supported call records include extracted property-loss details and transfer outcomes. We agree on data access, retention and privacy controls during setup.

Can it alert my on-call person by text or email?

Internal alert delivery can be configured where supported; we test it to your authorized destination before representing it as operational.

What if the on-call technician doesn't answer?

Failed handoffs must be recorded honestly. We configure approved backup and follow-up paths with you rather than promising someone answered when they did not.

What about an actual emergency?

The assistant recognizes clearly stated immediate danger and gives appropriate safety guidance. It does not replace emergency services or make structural, electrical or insurance determinations.
