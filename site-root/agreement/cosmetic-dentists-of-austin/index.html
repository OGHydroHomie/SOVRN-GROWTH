60 Smile Makeover and Full-Mouth Consults in 90 Days

01Parties and start date

This agreement is between Lux Eterna LLC ("SOVRN Growth") and The Cosmetic Dentists of Austin, the dental practice named at checkout (the "Practice"). It starts the day the Practice pays.

02What SOVRN Growth does

  • Builds and runs ad campaigns on Facebook and Instagram for the Practice's smile makeover and full-mouth cases, including the ads themselves.
  • Follows up with every person who responds, typically within 60 seconds, and screens each one against the booking criteria in section 05 before booking anyone.
  • Books screened candidates directly onto the Practice's calendar, with notes on the treatment they want, their timeline and their answer on financing.
  • Sends the Practice a weekly report of consults booked, and reviews results with the Practice on a call at day 30.

03What the Practice does

  • Pays Meta directly for ad spend, on its own card, at least $5,000 a month (about $165 a day) for the whole program. This can come out of the Practice's existing marketing budget. Ad spend is not part of SOVRN Growth's fee.
  • Gives SOVRN Growth access to its Meta ad account and its calendar or booking system within 2 business days of paying.
  • Approves or edits ads within 2 business days of receiving them.
  • Keeps consult times open and confirms booked consults within 1 business day.

04Fee and payment

The fee is $12,000, prepaid before work begins. Paying from a bank account is preferred; a card is also accepted. The fee is not refundable. If the target below isn't met, the guarantee is the Practice's remedy.

05The guarantee

If fewer than 60 qualifying consults are booked onto the Practice's calendar within 90 days of the ads going live, SOVRN Growth keeps working at no extra fee until 60 are booked. The guarantee holds as long as the Practice does its part above, including the minimum ad spend.

A qualifying consult is a consultation for a smile makeover or full-mouth treatment, booked through SOVRN Growth's ads or follow-up by a person who isn't already a patient of the Practice and who meets the booking criteria both parties agree in writing before the ads go live. The criteria cover the treatment the person wants, their timeline, and whether the Practice's price range or financing works for them. Until they're agreed, a person qualifies by saying they want one of these treatments, giving a timeline, and saying the Practice's price range or financing works for them. Each person counts once. Attendance, clinical suitability, financing approval and the decision to start treatment don't affect whether a booking counts.

06Timeline

  • Kickoff call within 1 business day of payment.
  • Ads go live within 7 days of receiving the access and ad approvals above.
  • The 90 days start the day the ads go live. Any work after that, like month-to-month service, is under a new agreement.

07Patient information and other terms

  • Patient information. SOVRN Growth keeps patient information confidential and will sign a Business Associate Agreement with the Practice before handling it.
  • Ownership. The Practice owns its ad accounts, its patient data and the ads made for it. SOVRN Growth keeps its own tools, templates and methods.
  • Ending early. The Practice may stop the program at any time; fees already paid are not refunded. If the Practice drops below the minimum ad spend or doesn't do its part, the guarantee ends.
  • Liability. SOVRN Growth's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fee paid. Neither party is liable for indirect damages or lost profits.
  • General. Texas law governs this agreement. It is the entire agreement between the parties, and any change must be in writing.

08How to accept

The Practice accepts this agreement by checking the agreement box on the SOVRN Growth payment page that links to it and paying the fee. There is nothing to print or sign separately; the payment page records who accepted and when.
