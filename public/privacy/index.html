Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

This page covers what SOVRN Growth collects when you contact us or when we handle calls, texts, and web leads on behalf of a heating and air company you reached out to, and what we do with it. Short version: we use it to respond to you and schedule work. We don't sell it.

SMS and mobile consent

Mobile information and consent to receive SMS will not be sold, rented, or shared with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, email address.
  • The content of calls, texts, and web forms you send.
  • Service details you share so someone can respond, like your address and what the system is doing.
  • Basic site usage information, like pages visited.

How we use it

  • To answer your calls, texts, and form fills.
  • To schedule, confirm, and follow up on appointments and estimates.
  • To run and improve the service for the company you contacted.

We share information only with the heating and air company you contacted and with the vendors needed to deliver messages and scheduling. We never sell it.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal, tax, and record-keeping requirements. After that it gets deleted or anonymized.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to opt out of texting.
  • Reply HELP to any text for assistance.
  • Email us to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you.
