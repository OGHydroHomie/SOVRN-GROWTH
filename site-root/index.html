Questions owners ask

Is Ryan a person?

No. Ryan is a voice agent we build for your company, with your name, your service area and your questions. If a caller wants a person, Ryan takes their number and alerts your on-call tech right away.

What if someone's in danger?

Ryan tells them to hang up and call 911. It never gives safety or repair instructions. That stays with your team.

Do I keep my number?

Yes. Your phone forwards calls to the line when your team doesn't pick up, or after hours. You choose.

What does it cost to run?

Usage on your own phone, voice and CRM accounts, billed by those vendors. You'll see the monthly number in writing before you sign.

Who hosts it?

Your choice. Own it outright on your accounts with nothing monthly to us, or we host it in a private account for $199 a month and move it to you any time.

We have more than one location.

Each extra office or market is $1,000 on either package and gets its own service area and on-call routing.
